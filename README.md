# GEM Loop: Time Capsule

> *Turn every visitor into a storyteller.*

A concept and working demo for the **Grand Egyptian Museum Hackathon (3rd Edition, 2025)**, where the project qualified for the participant stage.

## The problem

Museums talk to their visitors all day, but visitors rarely get an easy way to talk back. Feedback forms are boring, so most visitors skip them and the museum loses real insight into what people loved.

## The idea

Instead of asking visitors to fill in a form, GEM Loop turns feedback into a personal memory. Visitors capture the artifacts they care about, leave a video message for their future selves, and seal everything in a **Time Capsule** that unlocks on a later visit. While they enjoy the experience, the museum gathers feedback and engagement data without ever showing a questionnaire.

## How it works

1. **Smart ticketing:** the ticket carries the visitor's ID, which links the visit to their capsule.
2. **Photo Quest:** the visitor captures recommended artifacts (for example Ramses II, Tutankhamun, and the Khufu Ship). AI identifies the artifact and asks short, relevant micro-questions about the experience.
3. **Message to your future self:** a short video message and a personal goal are sealed in the capsule.
4. **Time Capsule Key + digital voucher:** the visitor gets a unique key to unlock the capsule on their next visit, plus a small reward.
5. **AI interests routing:** QR code scans and micro-question answers tell the museum which artifacts attract visitors.

## What the museum learns

| KPI | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Average dwell time per artifact | Which artifacts hold attention |
| Most scanned artifacts | Which exhibits spark interest |
| Engagement completion rate | How many visitors finish the experience |
| Visitor satisfaction with the hunt | Real-time feedback without forms |
| Number of new users engaged | Reach of the experience |

> The analytics dashboard screens in this repository use **sample (mock) data** to show what the museum would see. They are not real visitor results.
